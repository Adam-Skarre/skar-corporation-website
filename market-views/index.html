We examine those signals together, then ask the practical question: which decision changes?

Emerging market architecture

Markets are becoming an environment for agents.

Artificial intelligence is moving from analysis toward interpretation, allocation, and action. That shift changes the question from “What can the model predict?” to “How does a population of models behave when they observe the same information, share infrastructure, and act under the same constraints?”
