The thesis

A market story is not a market system.

The question is not whether excess exists. It is what is being financed, what cash flow supports it, which physical or financial constraint governs it, and what evidence would invalidate the prevailing view.

  1. 01Do not generalize from the loudest failure. Segment the market by structure, quality, and exposure.
  2. 02Follow the capital into the physical system. AI demand becomes real only through power, equipment, construction, and usable capacity.
  3. 03Full valuation is a condition, not a conclusion. Compare expectations with earnings, cash conversion, and delivery.

What would change our view

A thesis needs a failure condition.

Confidence should rise or fall with evidence. These are the observations that would force a serious reappraisal.

PRIVATE CREDIT

Credit stress broadens beyond isolated borrowers.

  • Defaults spread across sectors and vintages
  • Interest coverage deteriorates persistently
  • Recoveries fail to support structural assumptions
AI BUILDOUT

Capacity outruns productive demand.

  • Utilization remains weak after delivery
  • Power or grid delays destroy project economics
  • Revenue fails to convert from pilots to operations
PUBLIC EQUITIES

Delivery narrows while expectations remain high.

  • Earnings breadth deteriorates materially
  • Cash conversion separates from reported growth
  • Concentration amplifies a common disappointment
