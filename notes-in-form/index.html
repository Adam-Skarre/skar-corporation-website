Mathematics as a medium

Structure made visible.

Every image on this page begins as a relationship: an angle changing over time, a radius responding to another variable, a point inheriting the state of the point before it. Code turns those rules into motion. Thousands of marks accumulate into forms that feel organic, although every position is computed.

The commercial value appears when the same visual grammar is directed toward a question. Parametric systems can expose patterns in dense data, test geometries before anything is built, translate simulation into understandable form, and give digital products an identity generated from their own logic. At that intersection, mathematical art becomes a practice in data visualization, computational design, architectural form-finding, and interactive communication.
