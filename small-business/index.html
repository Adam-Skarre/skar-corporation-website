Decision situations

Build the structure growth now requires.

We focus on operating problems that consume owner attention, weaken delivery, or make the business harder to understand than it should be.

01

Owner bottleneck

Which decisions still wait for one person, and what knowledge has never become a dependable process?

Clarify roles, decision rights, escalation rules, essential routines, and the information others need to act.

02

Growth strains delivery

Why are scheduling, response time, quality, or customer commitments becoming harder to control?

Map demand and capacity, reveal queues and handoff failures, and redesign the flow around the real constraint.

03

Profit without cash clarity

Where is cash tied up between selling, performing the work, billing, collecting, and paying obligations?

Connect workload, pricing, margin, billing timing, receivables, payables, and near-term cash needs in one operating view.

04

Tool sprawl

Which software or automation will remove friction instead of creating another system to manage?

Define the workflow first, compare right-sized options, and sequence adoption around value and effort.
