A focused consulting model

From an unclear problem
to a usable decision.

Most engagements begin before the answer is obvious. SKAR clarifies the decision, tests the evidence, and leaves the client with something durable: a recommendation, model, roadmap, or working tool.

01

Frame the decision

Define the real question, the constraints that matter, who owns the outcome, and what a useful answer must accomplish.

02

Test the routes

Combine engineering analysis, scenarios, and operating evidence to expose consequences before resources are committed.

03

Make it usable

Deliver a clear recommendation and the reasoning behind it—along with the model, roadmap, or tool needed to act and adapt.

The work is scoped to the decision. Clients keep the logic, assumptions, and practical next steps—not a presentation that disappears after the meeting.
