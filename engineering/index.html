Engineering basis

Engineering decisions are systems decisions.

A product rarely fails because one equation was missing. It fails when requirements, interfaces, uncertainty, production, and operating reality were treated as separate conversations.

We build the technical basis that holds them together—so teams can see what governs performance, where risk concentrates, and which decision remains defensible when conditions change.
