Analytical discipline

A visible chain from observation to consequence.

Observe

Establish what is known.

Start with traceable evidence, conditions, definitions, and the limits of the available record.

Represent

Build the structure.

Express variables, constraints, interactions, and uncertainty in a form that can be examined.

Challenge

Find where the answer changes.

Test scenarios, sensitivities, and competing explanations instead of protecting a preferred conclusion.

Interpret

Connect analysis to action.

Show what the result means, what remains unresolved, and which decision it is capable of supporting.
