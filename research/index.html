Publication standard

A conclusion should be traceable.

Each brief documents a chain from the decision question to the evidence, method, interpretation, and limits of what can responsibly be concluded. SKAR synthesis is labelled as synthesis; public facts remain linked to their original source.

01

Source

Begin with authoritative, preferably primary, evidence.

02

Separate

Distinguish reported fact, calculation, and judgment.

03

Stress-test

Expose assumptions, ranges, sensitivity, and limitations.

04

Publish

Date the analysis and preserve a reviewable source trail.
