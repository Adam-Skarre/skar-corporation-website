    04 — The SKAR protocol

    From friction to forward motion.

    A disciplined path from a stubborn operating problem to a measurable change. Each stage produces a clear output before the next begins.

    I

    Discovery

    Map the work before prescribing the fix.

    SKAR AI reconstructs the workflow, decision rights, evidence, and delays that shape the current result.

    II

    Synthesis

    Separate the constraint from its symptoms.

    Signals are ranked, assumptions stay visible, and the limiting step is expressed as a testable diagnosis.

    III

    Intervention

    Give one owner the smallest useful move.

    SKAR AI proposes a bounded action, the approval it requires, and the measure that will show whether it worked.

    IV

    Evolution

    Turn the result into institutional learning.

    Outcomes update the operating model so the next diagnosis begins with stronger evidence and less uncertainty.
