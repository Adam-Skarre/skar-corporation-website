Decision situations

Where design intent begins to meet operating risk.

01

Design release

Which requirements, tolerances, interfaces, or critical characteristics remain unresolved before freeze?

Create a traceable requirements and trade-space view that makes open technical risk visible.

02

Production readiness

Can the process repeatedly achieve the intended result at the required rate?

Connect process flow, capacity, inspection, variation, tooling, and work instructions in one readiness basis.

Ramp & recovery

What is driving scrap, rework, cycle-time loss, or unstable output?

Separate symptoms from governing causes, prioritize countermeasures, and define evidence for closure.

Compare alternatives across performance, qualification effort, lead time, cost, continuity, and residual risk.
